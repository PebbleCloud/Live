Why it's different

Organise your world

Manage to do lists, save notes, and keep the ideas and reminders that matter close at hand.

Stay connected and inspired

Track your reading list, your watch list and the world you want to explore. Keep track of the people in your life: remember their birthdays, see when you last caught up and be gently reminded when it's time to rekindle connections.

Yours after the phone dies

Automatic backup is a safety net for a new phone; a one-tap manual export hands you a plain database file, kept wherever you want it — no subscription required to read your own life back.

Reflect on your life

Every module is designed to center you: your life, your experience, and what keeps your flame burning. Ember helps you see the bigger picture: the small moments that make life meaningful.
