Why it's different

Organise your world

Ember gives you nine dedicated spaces for different parts of your life. Whether you're tracking ideas, collecting recipes, or planning your future, there's a place for everything. All in one app, all on your device.

Stay connected and inspired

Keep photos, links, and notes together. Share moments with friends directly from the app. Stay inspired by revisiting your bucket list, reading list, or favorite movie recommendations whenever you need a pick-me-up.

Yours after the phone dies

No cloud sync means no dependence on servers. Your data lives safely on your phone, encrypted by Android's built-in security. If your device ever dies, you can export everything manually, on your own terms.

Reflect on your life

Write freely in your journal, revisit your achievements, and keep notes on the people who matter. Ember is designed to help you notice and appreciate the moments that make up your life.
