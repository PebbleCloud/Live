Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 19, 2026

Ember is developed by a small independent team. This privacy policy explains how we handle your data in the Ember app. We take your privacy seriously. If you have questions, please reach out to us.

The short version

  • We don't collect any data about you
  • No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking
  • No internet permission requested
  • Everything you store stays on your device
  • You have complete control over your data

1. Information the app stores

Ember stores information entirely on your device. This includes:

  • Lists and entries you create (notes, to-dos, bucket list items, reading lists, recipes)
  • Journal and log entries
  • People you track and their associated information
  • Tend check-ins and reflections
  • Photos you attach to entries

All of this data is stored in a private app directory on your Android device. The operating system isolates this storage so only Ember can access it. No data ever leaves your device automatically.

2. What the app does NOT do

Ember is built with privacy at its core. We do not:

  • Collect analytics or usage data
  • Track you across apps or websites
  • Share or sell your data to anyone
  • Use third-party services that would access your data
  • Require or use user accounts
  • Request network or internet permissions
  • Show ads or sponsored content

3. When data can leave your device

Data only leaves your device under your direct control:

  • Android device backup: If you have Android backups enabled through your Google account, your Ember data may be included in those backups (this is managed by Android, not Ember)
  • Manual export: You can export your data manually using the share sheet or export features built into Ember
  • Photos: If you choose to share a photo from Ember to another app or service, you are initiating that action

4. Data security

Your data is protected by:

  • Android's private app storage (data is isolated from other apps)
  • The security of your device itself
  • No transmission over the internet, so no interception risk

We recommend protecting your device with a lock screen password or biometric authentication. If you export Ember data, handle those files with care — they contain personal information.

5. Data retention and deletion

You have complete control over your data:

  • You can delete any entry, list, or piece of information at any time from within Ember
  • When you delete something, it's gone immediately
  • If you uninstall Ember, all app data is automatically deleted by Android
  • If you have Android backups, you can manage or restore those separately through Android settings
  • Any files you've exported are yours to manage — delete them anytime

6. Children and data privacy

Ember is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13. If a parent or guardian becomes aware that a child under 13 has provided information through Ember, please contact us and we will take steps to delete that information.

7. Your rights and control

You have complete control over your Ember data:

  • Read: You can view all the data you've stored in Ember at any time
  • Change: You can edit or update any information whenever you want
  • Export: You can export your data in various formats
  • Delete: You can delete any entry or the entire app and all associated data

8. Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy in the future, we will update this page and include a new "Last updated" date. If we make significant changes, we will notify users in the app or through release notes before the change becomes effective.

Contact us

If you have questions about this privacy policy or how Ember handles your data, please get in touch. Contact: support@emberapp.dev
